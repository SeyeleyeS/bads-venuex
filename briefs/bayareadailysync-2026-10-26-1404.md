## Where to eat
No Yelp ratings (Yelp blocks automated loads). Descriptions and hours are Michelin's unless noted; price signs are Michelin's.

**State Bird Provisions, Fillmore, SF (1529 Fillmore St) · Californian · $$$ · Michelin One Star.** Dim sum-style carts of seasonal California small plates, like dumplings, savory pancakes and liver mousse; the menu changes often. Reservations can be hard to get. Dinner daily 5:30–10 pm (10:30 Fri–Sat). Four blocks from the Fillmore for the Slow Pulp shows.
https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/state-bird-provisions

**Zuni Café, Hayes Valley/Market St, SF (1658 Market St) · Californian/French · $$$.** A San Francisco institution with a copper bar and wood-fired brick oven; Michelin says order oysters, the roast chicken for two and the gâteau Victoire. Closed Monday; Tue–Wed dinner 5–9:30 pm. Near the opera house for *Manon*.
https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/zuni-cafe

**Kin Khao, Union Square, SF (55 Cyril Magnin St) · Thai/Californian · $$$.** Michelin praises its layered, produce-driven Thai cooking and warns some dishes are very spicy. Its own site: lunch daily 11:30 am–2 pm, dinner Sun–Thu 5:30–9 pm (carried from the last brief). https://www.kinkhao.com/

**Nopalito, NoPa, SF (306 Broderick St) · Mexican · $$.** Blue-corn fish tacos, chilaquiles verdes, no reservations. Michelin lists 11:30 am–9 pm, except Wednesday 4:30–9 pm (carried). https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/nopalito

**Burdell, Oakland (4640 Telegraph Ave) · $$$.** Chef Geoff Davis's take on soul food. Closed Mon and Tue, so Wednesday only in this window (carried). https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/oakland/restaurant/burdell
