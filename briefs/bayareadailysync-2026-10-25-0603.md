## Where to eat
No Yelp ratings: Yelp still returns HTTP 403 to automated loads, so none are shown. Descriptions are from Michelin and the restaurants' own pages. Price signs are Michelin's.

**Kin Khao, Union Square, SF (55 Cyril Magnin St, in the Parc 55) · Thai/Californian · $$$.** Michelin praises Chef Pim Techamuanvivit's layered, produce-driven Thai cooking, like the hor mok curry mousse and a green curry with local rabbit, and warns some dishes are very spicy. Its own site: lunch daily 11:30 am–2 pm; dinner Sun–Thu 5:30–9 pm. (415) 362-7456. Handy before *Manon* or for Union Square hotel guests.
https://www.kinkhao.com/ · https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/kin-khao

**Nopalito, NoPa, SF (306 Broderick St) · Mexican/Californian · $$.** Michelin: a cheerful, sustainability-minded spot for blue-corn fish tacos, chilaquiles verdes and a must-order pulled-pork taco; save room for the concha. No reservations, so expect a wait. Michelin lists 11:30 am–9 pm daily except Wednesday (4:30–9 pm).
https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/nopalito

**Burdell, Temescal, Oakland (4640 Telegraph Ave) · contemporary American/Southern · $$$.** Michelin: Chef Geoff Davis's personal take on soul food, like berbere collards, pickle-brined fried chicken and chicken liver mousse with cornmeal waffle, plus a strong wine list. Michelin lists Sunday brunch 11 am–2 pm and dinner 5–8 pm; closed Mon and Tue.
https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/oakland/restaurant/burdell

**Still good from the last brief:** Anchor Oyster Bar (Castro) and Chez Panisse (Berkeley). Bombera (Oakland) and Tadich Grill are closed Sunday, and Bombera is also closed Tuesday.

## Getting around

**Muni and cable cars (re-checked tonight):** $2.85 single ride with Clipper, MuniMobile or tap-to-pay ($3.00 cash), good 120 minutes. Day pass $5.70. Cable car $9.00. Visitor Passports: 1-day $15, 3-day $35, 7-day $47. Youth 18 and under ride Muni free (not cable cars). https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/fares
**Muni reroutes:** Tue Oct 27, the 48 Quintara/24th St skips 24th & Sanchez, Church and Dolores stops 1:30–10 pm for the Noe Valley Night Market. SFMTA lists no other Sun–Tue event reroutes. Upcoming: Castro Halloween and other Oct 31 closures. https://www.sfmta.com/travel-updates

**BART:**
- **Sun Oct 25: no Green Line service.** Trains single-track between San Leandro and Bay Fair for vegetation work.
- **Tue Oct 27 – Fri Oct 30:** late-night single tracking on the Yellow Line in Contra Costa County.
- Coming up: reduced BART-to-OAK service the night of Nov 4, and a bus bridge between 24th St Mission and Daly City Nov 14–15.
- SFO: Sunday first airport train is 8:08 am, so plan another way for an early Sunday flight. Tap a contactless card or Clipper at the gate. Up to $2.85 off a transfer to another agency within 2 hours.
Sources: https://www.bart.gov/schedules/advisories · https://www.bart.gov/guide/airport

**Bikes and scooters (from the Oct 23 brief, not re-checked):** Lyft Bike (formerly Bay Wheels) is $1 to unlock plus $0.19/min classic or $0.49/min ebike, or a $15 day pass. Lime and Spin hold SF permits through June 2027. No sidewalk riding and no scooters on the Embarcadero Promenade. https://www.lyft.com/bikes/bay-wheels

**SFO cars and rides (from the Oct 23 brief):** Rental Car Center at 780 N. McDonnell Rd via AirTrain Blue Line (24 hours). Lyft and Uber pick up on Level 5 of the Domestic Garage. Taxis are at the Arrivals center islands. https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/ground-transportation

**Driving:** I-80 into SF and SR-37 reopen early Monday. Until then, use the San Mateo, Dumbarton or Richmond–San Rafael bridges.

## Where to stay
Per SF Travel, Union Square has most major hotels and is the transit hub (BART, Muni, cable cars), and it puts you near Kin Khao and the opera. Nob Hill is the upscale, romantic pick, and SoMa and Dogpatch are flat and walkable toward Chase Center. https://www.sftravel.com/neighborhoods
