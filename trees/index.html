How it works

Give directly to the planting partner.

  1. 1

    Open our fundraiser

    The donate button takes you to our fundraiser page on One Tree Planted's own fundraising site: [link placeholder].

  2. 2

    Donate there

    You pay One Tree Planted directly on their checkout. Bay Area Media doesn't see, hold or process your payment.

  3. 3

    One Tree Planted funds reforestation through its planting partners. Their projects include site analysis, seedlings, planting and a three-year monitoring period.
