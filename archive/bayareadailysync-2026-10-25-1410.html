Where to eat

No Yelp ratings: Yelp still returns HTTP 403 to automated loads, so none are shown. Descriptions are from Michelin and the restaurants' own pages. Price signs are Michelin's.

Kin Khao, Union Square, SF (55 Cyril Magnin St, in the Parc 55) · Thai/Californian · $$$. Michelin praises Chef Pim Techamuanvivit's layered, produce-driven Thai cooking, like the hor mok curry mousse and a green curry with local rabbit, and warns some dishes are very spicy. Its own site: lunch daily 11:30 am–2 pm; dinner Sun–Thu 5:30–9 pm. (415) 362-7456. Handy before Manon or for Union Square hotel guests.
https://www.kinkhao.com/ · https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/kin-khao

Nopalito, NoPa, SF (306 Broderick St) · Mexican/Californian · $$. Michelin: a cheerful, sustainability-minded spot for blue-corn fish tacos, chilaquiles verdes and a must-order pulled-pork taco; save room for the concha. No reservations, so expect a wait. Michelin lists 11:30 am–9 pm daily except Wednesday (4:30–9 pm).
https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/nopalito

Burdell, Temescal, Oakland (4640 Telegraph Ave) · contemporary American/Southern · $$$. Michelin: Chef Geoff Davis's personal take on soul food, like berbere collards, pickle-brined fried chicken and chicken liver mousse with cornmeal waffle, plus a strong wine list. Michelin lists Sunday brunch 11 am–2 pm and dinner 5–8 pm; closed Mon and Tue.
https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/oakland/restaurant/burdell

Still good from the last brief: Anchor Oyster Bar (Castro) and Chez Panisse (Berkeley). Bombera (Oakland) and Tadich Grill are closed Sunday, and Bombera is also closed Tuesday.

Where to stay
